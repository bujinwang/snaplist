How it works

Four steps, one photo in. Every step that can be automated is; the steps that need your judgement are left to you and clearly flagged.

Step 1

Shoot it flat

Lay the garment flat and snap one photo. No tripod, no lightbox, no props.

Step 2

We prettify

Background removal, straightening, and even lighting — on a neutral backdrop.

Step 3

Attributes & copy

Brand, colour, material, size, condition and measurements are detected and written into a title, description and keywords.

Step 4

Export

Download an eBay-ready CSV, copy-assist for Poshmark and Vinted, or copy the listing text to paste anywhere.

Honest claims

We would rather under-promise. Here is exactly what we can and cannot stand behind today, traced to our own planning docs.

No “time saved” number — on purpose

  • We do not publish a minutes-saved figure. Our metrics doc (docs/METRICS.md) contains zero measured values — every entry is a definition or a chosen threshold, explicitly marked UNMEASURED. Until that changes, any “you’ll save 30 minutes” line would be invented.
  • The manual baseline is an estimate, not a measurement. Listing one item by hand is roughly 8–19 minutes by our product estimate (PRD §3), most of it checking details, not typing. That estimate is unverified and we say so.
  • What we target (engineering, not a promise). The machine-ownable part of the flow — photo prep, attributes, export — has a design target of p50 ≤ 35 s, p90 ≤ 60 s per item (METRICS.md §3.3). That is a chosen threshold, not a measured result, and it deliberately excludes the time you spend reviewing the suggestion. We will not subtract it from your 8 minutes to manufacture a “<2 min” claim.
  • We never invent a detail. The copy generator will not state a brand or fibre the photo did not support (packages/shared/src/listing/copy.ts). If we didn’t detect it, the listing won’t claim it. You stay in control of price and category.

What’s in the asset pack

Everything the app produces for one item, ready to hand to a marketplace.

  • Prettified image(s) on a neutral backdrop
  • Detected category, colour, material, size, condition
  • Flat measurements (you confirm with a tape)
  • A title ≤ 80 characters, keyword-front-loaded
  • A structured description with a measurements block
  • Up to 10 search keywords
  • An eBay File Exchange CSV (header matches eBay’s template)
  • Poshmark & Vinted copy-assist rows (paste, not upload)
  • One-tap copy of title / description / keywords for any site
